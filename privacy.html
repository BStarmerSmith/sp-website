Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Who we are

Suzy Parker Games Limited (company number 16522095, registered office 36 Harvey Street, Folkestone, Kent, CT20 1LE) makes the games in the Suzy Parker Games suite, including The Middle Bit. We are the data controller for all of them, for the purposes of UK and EU GDPR.

Questions about your data, or want to exercise any of the rights below? Email privacy@suzyparkergames.co.uk.

One account, every game

You have one Suzy Parker Games account, and it works across every game we make. Your email address, display name, password and subscription are shared across all of them. What you've played — your progress and your streaks — is kept per game. That's why there is one privacy policy rather than one per title.

That last row is worth being precise about. When a puzzle is answered we record that it happened, which game it was, and how it ended. We attach no account, no session, no device identifier and no IP address — so it counts answers, not people. It is the reason we can tell you how many puzzles our games have served without building a profile of anyone.

We keep ordinary server logs, as any website does. Every request leaves a line recording the IP address it came from, what was asked for, the browser or app that asked, and the page it came from; and when someone tries to sign in, change a password or delete an account, the email address used is recorded too — that is what makes a log useful if an account is attacked. We don't use them to build a profile, and we don't join them to what you've played.

Why we're allowed to do this

We handle your account, progress and subscription because we need to in order to provide the game you asked for. We show personalised ads only where you've consented. If you want that consent changed or withdrawn, email privacy@suzyparkergames.co.uk and we'll act on it. We keep server logs and anonymous play counts because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the games secure and knowing how much they're played.

What we don't do

  • We don't use third-party analytics. No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, nothing of that kind, on our website or in our games.
  • We don't collect crash reports or diagnostics from your device.
  • We don't build an advertising profile of you. We don't sell personal data, and we never have.
  • We never see your card details. Payments are handled entirely by the companies listed below.

What our games keep on your device is only what they need to work: your sign-in tokens, your account details and progress so the game loads instantly, a few interface preferences, and — if you play without an account — that day's progress and a note of which puzzles have already been counted, so one answer isn't counted twice. Nothing there is shared with anyone, and clearing your browser data removes all of it.

Who else sees your data

This is the complete list:

  • RevenueCat and Stripe — managing subscriptions and taking payment.
  • Apple and Google Play — purchases made inside our mobile apps.
  • Google AdSense and AdMob — the ads shown to players without Premium.
  • Amazon SES — delivering our emails, such as password resets.
  • Cloudflare — hosting this website.
  • Hetzner — the hosting company whose servers our games and your account data run on.
  • Google Fonts — the typeface this site and our games use. Your browser fetches it from Google, which means Google sees your IP address when a page loads.
  • Expo — delivering updates to our mobile apps. Each time you open one it checks Expo for a newer version, which means Expo sees your IP address, your platform and which version you're running.

Your account and game data sits on servers we run ourselves, rented from the hosting company named above. Some of the companies listed operate outside the UK and EEA; where that happens, standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards apply.

Where the law requires it — in the UK and the EEA, and anywhere else with the same rule — you'll be asked about ad tracking the first time you play, and we show no ads until you've answered. Elsewhere no prompt is required and none is shown, so ads can appear straight away; we still show nothing until the consent step has finished running. Premium subscribers never see any ads, anywhere. Wherever you are, you can email privacy@suzyparkergames.co.uk to object to personalised advertising.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Here is exactly what happens.

Straight away: you're signed out everywhere and the account stops working. You can't sign in to it, on any of our games. We email you confirming the date it will be erased, which is 30 days later.

For those 30 days: nothing has been erased yet. Changed your mind? Email support@suzyparkergames.co.uk before that date and we'll restore the account with everything intact. We send one reminder three days before the deadline.

After 30 days: your account, progress, streaks, votes and subscription record are deleted from our database outright — removed, not hidden or flagged. This cannot be undone. Your email address becomes free to register with again, so you're welcome back any time.

Three things deliberately remain, and we'd rather say so than imply otherwise. The anonymous count that a puzzle was answered stays, because it contains nothing that identifies you — removing it would quietly rewrite how much our games have been played. If you created a puzzle or an invite code, that stays too, with the link to you removed. And the server logs described above are not rewritten: lines already written keep the email address they recorded, so a sign-in or a password change from before you deleted the account can still be seen in them until those logs rotate out, which may be after the 30 days are up.

Payment records aren't ours to delete. Apple, Google, Stripe and RevenueCat each keep their own transaction records, for their own legal and tax reasons, under their own policies.

Backups. We back the database up every night and keep 30 nights' worth. Because erasure happens 30 days after you ask for it, and a backup taken the day before that can then sit for another 30 nights, data can persist in a backup for up to about 60 days from the moment you press delete. After that it rolls off and is gone.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to send it to you in a portable form, or to restrict or object to how we use it. Email privacy@suzyparkergames.co.uk and we'll sort it out — we'll always reply within a month. A copy of your data is available on request.

If you think we've handled your data badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd rather you told us first so we can put it right.

Age

You need to be at least 13 to hold an account, or older if your country sets a higher minimum. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.
