Who we are

Suzy Parker Games Limited (company number 16522095, registered office 36 Harvey Street, Folkestone, Kent, CT20 1LE) makes the games in the Suzy Parker Games suite, including The Middle Bit. We are the data controller for all of them, for the purposes of UK and EU GDPR.

Questions about your data, or want to exercise any of the rights below? Email privacy@suzyparkergames.co.uk.

One account, every game

You have one Suzy Parker Games account, and it will work across every game we make. Your email address, display name, password and subscription are shared across all of them. All your game data, including your progress and streaks, is kept per game in one central Suzy Parker Games account. That is why there is one privacy policy rather than one per title.

Today our games are played in a web browser. Apps for iPhone and Android are on the way. Where this policy describes the apps, it is telling you now what will happen when they launch - none of it is happening today.

Why we're allowed to do this

We handle your account, progress and subscription because we need to in order to provide the game and the mechanics that make it work. Where a consent form is required - in the UK and the EEA, and anywhere else with the same rule - personalised ads are shown only if you agreed to them. It is worth being plain about how that works, because none of it is ours: Google shows the form, Google stores your answer, and Google applies it when it serves the ad. We never see what you chose, and there is no setting in our code that turns personalisation on or off. Where no form is required you won't see one, and we rely instead on our legitimate interest in funding free games with advertising.

Changing your mind, or objecting. If a consent form was shown to you, you can reopen it and change your answer at any time from Settings. If one wasn't, and you would rather we didn't show you advertising on a legitimate-interest basis, email privacy@suzyparkergames.co.uk and we'll act on it. Premium removes advertising entirely.

Keeping your play after you delete your account rests on that same legitimate interest rather than on your account, which by then no longer exists: an accurate record of how our puzzles have actually been played is worth having, and deleting it would quietly change the past. It is the reason we cut the link to you at the same moment - the record is worth keeping, and who made it is not.

What we don't do

  • We don't use third-party analytics. No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, nothing of that kind, on our website or in our games.
  • We don't collect crash reports or diagnostics from your device.
  • We don't build an advertising profile of you. We don't sell personal data, and we never have.
  • We never see your card details. Payments are handled entirely by the companies listed below.

What our games keep on your device is only what they need to work: your sign-in tokens, your account details and progress so the game loads instantly, a few interface preferences, and if you play without an account, that day's progress and a note of which puzzles have already been counted, so one answer isn't counted twice. On the web, clearing your browser data removes all of it. In the mobile apps, when they launch, sign-in tokens will be held in the device's own secure storage, which clearing data inside the app does not touch - signing out will remove them, and so will uninstalling the app.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. If you can't get into your account you can also ask us to delete it by emailing support@suzyparkergames.co.uk. Here is exactly what happens.

Straight away: you're signed out everywhere and the account stops working. You can't sign in to it, on any of our games. We email you confirming the date it will be erased, which is 30 days later.

For those 30 days: nothing has been erased yet. Changed your mind? Email support@suzyparkergames.co.uk before that date and we'll restore the account with everything intact.

After 30 days: your account and your subscription record are deleted from our database outright. Removed, not hidden or flagged. This cannot be undone. Your email address becomes free to register with again, so you're welcome back any time as a fresh new user.

What you played is kept, but it stops being yours. Your progress, streaks, votes and the guesses you typed are not deleted. Instead the link to your account is cut: every one of those rows is stamped with a fresh random identifier that belongs to nobody, generated at the moment of deletion and never written down anywhere that names you. The play still hangs together - the streak is still that streak, the guesses still sit against the puzzles they were made on - so it could have been anyone, and we no longer know who. We do this because deleting it would quietly rewrite how much our games have been played, and because once the name is gone there is nothing in it that points back to a person.

Four things deliberately remain, and we'd rather say so than imply otherwise. Your gameplay stays, cut loose from you as described above. The anonymous record of a puzzle being answered stays, for the same reason and on the same terms. The server logs described above are not rewritten: lines already written keep the email address they recorded, so a sign-in or a password change from before you deleted the account can still be seen in them until those logs rotate out, which may be after the 30 days are up. Finally, email you sent us stays in the mailbox it arrived in; tell us you want it gone too and we'll delete it.

Payment records aren't ours to delete. Stripe and RevenueCat each keep their own transaction records, for their own legal and tax reasons, under their own policies. Apple and Google will do the same for anything bought in the mobile apps once those launch.

Backups. We back the database up every night and keep 30 nights' worth. Because erasure happens 30 days after you ask for it, and a backup taken the day before that can then sit for another 30 nights, data can persist in a backup for up to about 60 days from the moment you press delete. After that it rolls off and is gone.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to send it to you in a portable form, or to restrict or object to how we use it. Email privacy@suzyparkergames.co.uk and we'll sort it out - we'll always reply within a month. A copy of your data is available on request.

Asking us to delete your data does the same thing as deleting your account yourself, and we'd rather be straight about the limit of it: your account and your subscription record go, and your play is kept with the link to you cut, exactly as described above.

If you think we've handled your data badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd rather you told us first so we can put it right.
